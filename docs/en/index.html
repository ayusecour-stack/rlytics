Every player’s tracker

As soon as the match starts, RLytics opens the tracker page of every player: 2, 4 or 6 squares depending on the mode. Click a player to see them in full, or open them in your browser. You can also see one team at a time in full screen (the 3 Blues, then the 3 Oranges).

Ranks in the Tab overlay

Hold Tab (or your controller button): the rank logo, division and MMR show up next to each player, for the mode you’re playing. Press F9 to see their 1v1, 2v2 or 3v3 ranks.

How it works

  1. Install RLytics. Windows may show “Windows protected your PC”: the app doesn’t have a paid signature. Click “More info”, then “Run anyway”.
  2. Start Rocket League in borderless windowed mode. If the game’s Stats API isn’t enabled yet, RLytics offers to enable it in one click (restart the game once).
  3. Play. As soon as the match starts, every player’s tracker shows up. The first time, tracker.gg asks for your cookie choice: do it once, it’s remembered.
  4. Hold Tab in a match. Everyone’s ranks show up on your scoreboard, already well placed in 1v1, 2v2 and 3v3. If they’re off on yours (different interface size…), one photo of the scoreboard in the settings is enough to fix them.

Frequently asked questions

Windows shows “Windows protected your PC”, is that normal?

Yes. Windows shows this message for programs without a paid signature (a certificate costs several hundred euros per year). Click “More info”, then “Run anyway”. It only shows up on install: automatic updates don’t trigger it.

Can I get banned?

RLytics doesn’t touch the game: it injects nothing, doesn’t read its memory and doesn’t even open its process. It reads Rocket League’s official Stats API (made by Psyonix for overlays) and shows a window on top of the game, like Discord or OBS. There’s no official guarantee from Psyonix, but it works the same way as allowed overlays.

Where do the ranks come from?

From the public pages of rocketleague.tracker.network, which RLytics shows like a browser. The Tab ranks are read from these already open pages, with no extra requests. RLytics isn’t affiliated with Tracker Network: if their site changes, an RLytics update may be needed.

Does it work on Steam and Epic? With a controller?

Yes to both. For a controller, pick the button that opens your scoreboard in the settings. PlayStation, Xbox and Switch players in your match show up too.

Does it slow down my game?

Pages load once at the start of the match, then they’re paused (still image) while Rocket League is in front, and theme animations stop during matches. If your PC is tight, turn off “Load trackers automatically” in the settings.

Do I need an account? Where does my data go?

No account. Everything stays on your PC: settings, history, notes on players, your MMR curve, rank positions. RLytics only connects to tracker.gg (player pages), AniList (anime theme images) and GitHub (updates). One exception: a comment or an idea you write yourself in Settings → Your feedback goes to the creator when you press “Send” (with the app version, the language and a random anonymous number, so they can answer you).

Is RLytics available in French?

Yes: Settings → Language. In “Auto”, RLytics follows your Windows language.

How do I get new versions?

Automatically: RLytics checks at startup, then every 3 hours, downloads the new version in the background and installs it when you close it (or right away with the “Restart” button).

I found a bug or have an idea, what do I do?

The easiest way: in RLytics, Settings → Your feedback → Comment (or Suggest an idea). The creator reads everything and answers you in the same place. For a bug, the log helps too: Settings → About → “Open the log”. This file explains what happened (it stays on your PC, nothing is sent). Describe the problem on the issues page and attach the log if you want.
